❓ FAQ

What is TokenMost?

tokenmost.com is an intelligence site for free AI token and credit offers, aggregating deals from LLM vendors (Zhipu, DeepSeek, Kimi, iFlytek, MiniMax), agent platforms, MaaS clouds and resellers. Every offer is verified daily and carries a verification date; expired offers are removed automatically.

What does "DS Flash equivalent" mean?

All quotas are normalized to DeepSeek V4.1 Flash equivalent tokens using its off-peak price (¥1.75/M domestic, $0.2625/M international), so offers from different vendors are directly comparable.

How are the value numbers calculated?

First-month reference = one-time + daily allowance within its valid campaign days (up to 30) + monthly allowance. Paid subscriptions, unverified offers and credits without a fixed ratio are excluded from the verified total.

Is the data trustworthy?

Every offer is verified against official pages, console tests or multiple independent sources, labeled with verification status (verified today / pending re-check / expired). Stale entries are removed automatically; limited-time events show deadlines.

Where are the discount codes?

Open the Coupon tab to view and copy codes per platform. Some are community-shared; always confirm validity on the vendor's official page.

How often is data updated?

Dual-track: automated monitoring of official pricing/campaign pages + human review before publishing. Expired offers are cleaned automatically. Heavy monitoring around major events (1024, Double 11, weekends). Subscribe to RSS for instant updates.

Auto+manual daily updates · offers subject to official pages
