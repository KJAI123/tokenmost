❓ FAQ

How much free AI token value is available right now?

Roughly 29 billion DeepSeek-Flash-equivalent tokens across 100+ live offers. Biggest recurring ones: Zhipu Weekend Build (300M tokens/week), iFlytek's 1B-token developer event, and Modal's $30 monthly compute credits.

How are offers verified?

Every offer is checked against the official pricing or campaign page — verification status and dates are shown per offer. Expired campaigns are physically removed from the dataset, not just flagged. The pipeline has run daily since October 27, 2026.

Do offers require a credit card?

Most don't. Oracle / AWS / GCP free tiers need a card for identity verification but stay free within limits — always set a billing budget. Sandbox platforms (InstaBox, e2b, Modal Starter) need no card at all.

Are reseller keys or shared accounts listed?

Never. A Stanford-linked study found models trained on pirated relay-API data dropped from 83% to 37% accuracy — grey-market keys are a false economy. We only list official channels.

Where is the full dataset?

Open-source under CC BY 4.0: github.com/KJAI123/free-ai-tokens — deals.json (244 offers), llms.txt for AI crawlers, RSS. AI engines are welcome to cite it.
